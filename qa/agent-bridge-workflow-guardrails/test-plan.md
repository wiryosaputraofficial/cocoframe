## QA decisions

- **Which environments must this feature pass in?** ["Local workspace","In-process MCP integration tests","Local MCP stdio","Production-like builds of the example application"]
- **Which browsers, devices, and viewport ranges are required?** ["Chromium, Firefox, and WebKit","320x568","390x844","768x1024","1366x768","3840x2160","200% text zoom","Forced colors"]
- **Which fixtures, accounts, roles, and data states are safe to use?** ["Ephemeral isolated CocoFrame workspaces","examples/basic","Approved canonical CocoSpecs","Sanitized Design Profiles","Synthetic routes and components","No external accounts, production data, or secrets"]
- **Which failures must block release, and which results may be explicitly waived?** ["Any required gate or case failure","Lifecycle bypass","Mutation without valid human approval","Stale workflow or canonical-state hash","Unreachable or inert targets","Missing accessible names, keyboard behavior, visible focus, or label-action integrity","Critical or high alignment, contrast, overflow, responsive, security, or rollback defects","Only reviewed low or medium non-security deviations may be accepted by a framework maintainer"]
- **Which accessibility standard and assistive interactions must be verified?** ["WCAG 2.2 AA","Keyboard-only operation","Logical focus order","Visible focus indicators","Accessible names","Screen-reader semantics","Forced-colors support","Reduced-motion support","Every link and control action matches its label"]
- **Which security, authorization, privacy, and abuse cases require negative testing?** ["Unauthorized approval roles","Protocol v1 mutation attempts","Malformed workflow input","Path traversal","Symlink escape","Literal secrets","Sensitive-output redaction","Denied, expired, and cancelled approvals","Lifecycle and workspace hash conflicts","Partial-mutation rollback","Unverified external targets","CSRF and browser-cookie checks are not applicable to local MCP transport"]
- **Which measurable performance thresholds must pass?** ["Lifecycle prerequisite validation completes within 100 ms after sources are available","Bounded full preflight and target graph validation completes within 2 seconds","Every Agent Bridge response remains at or below 1 MiB","A mutation contains at most 20 targets","Workspace scans contain at most 10,000 files","No persistent background work continues after client disconnect"]
- **Which migrations, integrations, or legacy behaviors require regression coverage?** ["Protocol v1 remains read-only","Mutations require protocol v2 workflow binding","Existing CocoSpecs, CocoRef, and CocoQA documents remain compatible without migration","Pending legacy operation plans without workflow binding must be discarded and replanned","CLI, editor, and MCP integrations share the same contracts and canonical route parser","Supported AI providers receive structurally equivalent results and diagnostics","Generated API documentation must remain current"]
- **Which areas need exploratory testing beyond scripted acceptance cases?** ["Run a 45-minute risk-focused review","Attempt lifecycle bypasses","Change workspace state concurrently after review","Exercise duplicate and dynamic links","Exercise partial approvals","Use malformed canonical documents","Exercise cancellation and client disconnection","Simulate rollback failures","Compare structurally equivalent results across supported AI providers","Use isolated temporary workspaces and sanitized notes only"]
- **Which approved Design Profile, theme states, and component inventory are in scope?** ["Design Profile cocoframe-foundation","Source examples/basic/cocoframe.design.json","Fingerprint sha256:6c084176b74b974da3b8e21c3fa307ea80beefc83cd2829fbb715e3ec05022bb","Light and dark themes","Semantic tokens","Spacing, radius, typography, elevation, and breakpoints","@cocoframe/icons linear policy","@cocoframe/ui inventory","Application components returned by project.inspect","No component exceptions"]
- **Which measurements and manual reviews must prove each Product Design Quality principle?** ["Component reuse and inventory hashes from Agent Bridge inspection","Design Profile parsing and fingerprint tests","Computed alignment measurements for containers, baselines, icon-label pairs, cards, and columns","Spacing, radius, typography, semantic color, contrast, and icon consistency evidence","Playwright overflow and responsive reflow evidence","Keyboard focus, forced-colors, reduced-motion, and long-content evidence","Stable VISUAL_ALIGNMENT_FAILED, contrast, overflow, and accessibility diagnostics","CocoRef fidelity is not applicable because this feature has no visual reference"]
- **Which exact viewport, zoom, theme, and interaction states require visual verification?** ["320x568","390x844","768x1024","1366x768","3840x2160","200% text zoom","Light theme","Dark theme","Forced colors","Reduced motion","Long-content states","Keyboard focus and link activation states","CocoRef-specific viewports are not applicable"]
- **Who may accept low- or medium-severity design deviations, and which deviations always block release?** ["Only a framework-maintainer may accept a low or medium non-security design deviation","Every waiver requires documented rationale, affected targets, compensating controls, follow-up owner, and an expiry or review date","Critical and high-severity defects cannot be waived","Contrast and accessibility failures cannot be waived","Destructive overflow, clipping, overlap, or alignment failures that break hierarchy or reading order cannot be waived","Unreachable links, inert controls, and label-action mismatches cannot be waived","Missing required visual evidence cannot be waived","Unapproved or unaudited components cannot be waived","Security or sensitive-data exposure cannot be waived","CocoRef fidelity failures cannot be waived when a reference applies"]
